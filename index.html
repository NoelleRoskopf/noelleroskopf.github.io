About Me

I am an aspiring instructional designer with a Master of Science in Curriculum and Instruction and a background in education, curriculum design, and learner-centered teaching. I am transitioning into instructional design and eLearning development by creating interactive training experiences that apply adult learning principles, scenario-based learning, clear learning objectives, and engaging visual design.

My goal is to design meaningful learning experiences that help learners build confidence, apply new skills, and succeed in real-world workplace situations.
