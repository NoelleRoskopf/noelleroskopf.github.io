Featured Project 01

Think Before You Click: Cybersecurity Awareness Challenge

An interactive cybersecurity awareness course that helps employees recognize common digital threats and make safer decisions in realistic workplace situations.

👤
My Role Instructional Designer and eLearning Developer
🎯
Audience Employees in a corporate workplace
🖥️
Delivery Self-paced web-based training

⚠️ The Challenge

Employees encounter phishing emails, unsafe links, suspicious attachments, and social engineering tactics. They need practical training that helps them recognize warning signs and respond appropriately.

💡 The Solution

I created an interactive Articulate Rise 360 course that places learners in realistic workplace situations and asks them to evaluate threats, make decisions, and receive immediate feedback.

🧠 Learning Strategy

The course uses scenario-based learning, retrieval practice, decision-making activities, knowledge checks, visual cues, and immediate feedback to reinforce safe behaviors.

🎨 Design Decisions

Content is organized into short, focused sections to reduce cognitive load. Interactions are used intentionally to support application without overwhelming the learner.

🎓 Learning Objectives

  • Recognize phishing and social engineering warning signs.
  • Evaluate suspicious emails, links, and attachments.
  • Choose an appropriate response to a potential threat.

📈 Business Value

The course supports safer employee behavior, reduces avoidable security risks, and strengthens a culture of cybersecurity awareness.

Featured Project 02

Communicating Effectively in the Workplace

An interactive eLearning course that helps employees strengthen professional communication skills and apply effective strategies in workplace situations.

👤
My Role Instructional Designer and eLearning Developer
🎯
Audience Employees and emerging professionals
🖥️
Delivery Self-paced web-based training

⚠️ The Challenge

Workplace misunderstandings can result from unclear messages, poor channel selection, weak listening skills, and an inappropriate professional tone.

💡 The Solution

I developed a structured Rise 360 course that teaches learners to select suitable communication channels, practice active listening, write professional emails, and communicate clearly.

🧠 Learning Strategy

The course uses scenarios, examples and non-examples, flashcards, accordion interactions, knowledge checks, and reflection to support practical application.

🎨 Design Decisions

Each lesson focuses on one core communication skill and builds toward workplace application. Interactions support engagement while keeping the learning objectives central.

🎓 Learning Objectives

  • Select an appropriate communication channel.
  • Apply active listening strategies.
  • Use a clear and professional workplace tone.

📈 Business Value

The course supports clearer communication, stronger teamwork, fewer misunderstandings, and more professional workplace interactions.
